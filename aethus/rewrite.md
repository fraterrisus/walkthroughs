Make sure you assign the [Mining Explosives]() to a quickbar slot before you enter the caves. Then head over to the NW corner and find the rockfall again. Toss the explosives at the rocks just like you would a [Terraforming Charge]() and they should clear out of the way. It's not impossible to get them all with one toss, but it's more likely to require two or three.

Roland identifies [Silica](), a new element among the remnants left behind in the explosion. This also leads him to inform you that you'll need to build a [Trade Node]() in order to connect to the ARC mercantile network and sell all the shiny objects you're finding for credits. Credits, in turn, are how you're going to purchase upgrades, such as larger oxygen tanks for Maeve and more powerful mining lasers for Roland.

On the far side of the rockfall you'll come across a locked crate with a datapad on top of it. The datapad contains another short journal entry from Ms. Li, who seems determined to blow up *something* with a handful of Mining Explosives. Sure enough, if you had the code — it's `7878` — and could open the crate right now, you'd find three Explosives and a [Terraforming Charge](). But the journal entry where you learn the code is a long way from here, and by the time you learn it the contents of this crate aren't useful in any way, so I recommend you use it right now. This is the joy of using a walkthrough, after all.

You're still operating on limited air here, so hug the wall to your right and circle around a little bit to the North. Sure enough, there's another exit from the caverns here ("Side Cave Entrance" on the map) and if you stepped outside you'd find yourself at the alternate entrance that Roland told you to skip when we first arrived here. Oops. Anyway, much like the first cave entrance, this one acts as a spot to refill your oxygen, so you can chill out here for a second and survey the area around you while your tanks recharge.

There are a bunch of new nodes in this region, along with the brown/orange Regolith nodes you've seen before. The pale blue nodes off to your right (NW) are Kaloxite Nodes, which produce [Kaloxite Ore](), [Oxite](), and [Silica](). The white nodes are Kalynite Nodes — if you're like me you'll spend at least half the game getting those two backwards — which produce [Kalynite Ore](), [Solazine](), and [Silica](). Every kind of node you find in this area also produces [Sandstone](), [Dust Rubies](), and [Pangimony]() when you mine them. You may also spot some red Dust Ruby Nodes. Check out the [mining page](/aethus/mining) for a reference guide to what you'll find in each area. 

There's also some new plants to be harvested, besides the Glowtus, Cacti, and Cave Mushrooms you already know about. Korvo Plants are purple, flat, and have broad leaves; they produce [Leaves](/aethus/items/#korvo-leaf) and [Seeds](/aethus/items/#korvo-seed). However they tend to float on top of toxic swamp water, so you will learn to harvest them from afar. Finally, you'll come across a series of circular stone "nests" that sometimes contain [Mysterious Eggs](). We'll talk more about those in a bit.

The elephant in the room, though, is that large mining facility that's up on top of the hill South of where you're standing. Go ahead and run over there now; you should be able to spot the front door from where you're standing. The good news is that the generator is functional, the airlocks are tight, and the habitat is pressurized, so you can also use it as a source for oxygen. In fact... I tend to agree with Roland. It's awfully lucky to find a place that's kitted out this well, and that much luck is a bit too creepy to really be celebrated.

> #4 — Clifftop Living

The game doesn't formally name this building, but we'll call it **Facility Alpha**, for reasons that will become clear shortly. Check the place out at your leisure. There' s a working [Regolith Printer]() here in case you prefer to haul less [Regolith]() and more [Building Materials]() back to your outpost. There's also a working [Crafting Bench](). Despite the proximity of raw materials, I never found crafting facilities like this to be useful, because they only have access to whatever's currently in your inventory as opposed to your entire outpost's storage units. Maybe you will though?

On a shelf opposite those you'll find a [Nutrient Bar](), a [Cargo Drone]() that will become super important shortly, and a workpad with a journal entry introducing some incoming staff members. (Remember, you're reading these in retrospect.) **Ncuti Sinclar** and **Caleb Vihaan** are names that will pop up several times as we learn more about what happened here, but perhaps more intriguing is the implication that there are actually *several* facilities in this complex and they're named for Greek letters. (In case you're wondering, Theta is the eighth letter in the alphabet and Lambda is eleventh. But Alpha is first, which is why I've dubbed this Facility Alpha.)

Other things you can scavenge here include another [Mining Explosive]() on the shelf, a [Kalynite Frame]() in the locker, an [Oxygen Dome]() on the dining table, an [ARC Credit Chip](), and one unit of [Basic Electronics]() in the lockbox. There are also two sets of Bunk Beds here, which you can totally use to sleep in case you don't want to return to your outpost for some reason. Again, I never once took advantage of these, but maybe you will. Finally, a [Suit Charger]() sits along one wall; hit the link for more on what they're used for.
