Make sure you assign the [Mining Explosives]() to a quickbar slot before you enter the caves. Then head over to the NW corner and find the rockfall again. Toss the explosives at the rocks just like you would a [Terraforming Charge]() and they should clear out of the way. It's not impossible to get them all with one toss, but it's more likely to require two or three.

Roland identifies [Silica](), a new element among the remnants left behind in the explosion. This also leads him to inform you that you'll need to build a [Trade Node]() in order to connect to the ARC mercantile network and sell all the shiny objects you're finding for credits. Credits, in turn, are how you're going to purchase upgrades, such as larger oxygen tanks for Maeve and more powerful mining lasers for Roland.

On the far side of the rockfall you'll come across a locked crate with a datapad on top of it. The datapad contains another short journal entry from Ms. Li, who seems determined to blow up *something* with a handful of Mining Explosives. Sure enough, if you had the code — it's `7878` — and could open the crate right now, you'd find three Explosives and a [Terraforming Charge](). But the journal entry where you learn the code is a long way from here, and by the time you learn it the contents of this crate aren't useful in any way, so I recommend you use it right now. This is the joy of using a walkthrough, after all.

You're still operating on limited air here, so hug the wall to your right and circle around a little bit to the North. Sure enough, there's another exit from the caverns here ("Side Cave Entrance" on the map) and if you stepped outside you'd find yourself at the alternate entrance that Roland told you to skip when we first arrived here. Oops. Anyway, much like the first cave entrance, this one acts as a spot to refill your oxygen, so you can chill out here for a second and survey the area around you while your tanks recharge.

There are a bunch of new nodes in this region, along with the brown/orange Regolith nodes you've seen before. The pale blue nodes off to your right (NW) are Kaloxite Nodes, which produce [Kaloxite Ore](), [Oxite](), and [Silica](). The white nodes are Kalynite Nodes — if you're like me you'll spend at least half the game getting those two backwards — which produce [Kalynite Ore](), [Solazine](), and [Silica](). Every kind of node you find in this area also produces [Sandstone](), [Dust Rubies](), and [Pangimony]() when you mine them. You may also spot some red Dust Ruby Nodes. Check out the [mining page](/aethus/mining) for a reference guide to what you'll find in each area. 

There's also some new plants to be harvested, besides the Glowtus, Cacti, and Cave Mushrooms you already know about. Korvo Plants are purple, flat, and have broad leaves; they produce [Leaves](/aethus/items/#korvo-leaf) and [Seeds](/aethus/items/#korvo-seed). However they tend to float on top of toxic swamp water, so you will learn to harvest them from afar. Finally, you'll come across a series of circular stone "nests" that sometimes contain [Mysterious Eggs](). We'll talk more about those in a bit.

The elephant in the room, though, is that large mining facility that's up on top of the hill South of where you're standing. Go ahead and run over there now; you should be able to spot the front door from where you're standing. The good news is that the generator is functional, the airlocks are tight, and the habitat is pressurized, so you can also use it as a source for oxygen. In fact... I tend to agree with Roland. It's awfully lucky to find a place that's kitted out this well, and that much luck is a bit too creepy to really be celebrated.

> #4 — Clifftop Living

The game doesn't formally name this building, but we'll call it **Facility Alpha**, for reasons that will become clear shortly. Check the place out at your leisure. There' s a working [Regolith Printer]() here in case you prefer to haul less [Regolith]() and more [Building Materials]() back to your outpost. There's also a working [Crafting Bench](). Despite the abundance of raw materials nearby, I never found crafting facilities like this to be useful, because they only have access to whatever's currently in your inventory as opposed to your entire outpost's storage units. Maybe you will though?

On a shelf opposite those you'll find a [Nutrient Bar](), a [Cargo Drone]() that will become super important shortly, and a workpad with a journal entry introducing some incoming staff members. (Remember, you're reading these in retrospect.) **Ncuti Sinclar** and **Caleb Vihaan** are names that will pop up several times as we learn more about what happened here, but perhaps more intriguing is the implication that there are actually *several* facilities in this complex and they're named for Greek letters. (In case you're wondering, Theta is the eighth letter in the alphabet and Lambda is eleventh. But Alpha is first, which is why I've dubbed this Facility Alpha.)

Other things you can scavenge here include another [Mining Explosive]() on the shelf, a [Kalynite Frame]() in the locker, an [Oxygen Dome]() on the dining table, an [ARC Credit Chip](), and one unit of [Basic Electronics]() in the lockbox. There are also two sets of Bunk Beds here, which you can totally use to sleep in case you don't want to return to your outpost for some reason. Again, I never once took advantage of these, but maybe you will. Finally, a [Suit Charger]() sits along one wall; hit the link for more on what they're used for.

Go out the side door of Alpha and you'll find a small set of platforms. To your right are two huge Storage Containers, which make excellent staging areas for mining runs (600kg capacity!). There's also a smaller crate, and the blueprint for making [Medium Crates]() of your own back home, which is a nice boost to your storage capabilities. One of the Containers has two Mining Explosives in it, and the smaller Crate has a [Health Injector](). Hang on to that.

On the other side of the platforms is an elevator with a breathtaking view of the area beyond, which turns out to be the same giant crater that you found on the surface. Maeve says she can't see through the dust, but if you're here during the daytime you ought to be able to make out another mining habitat deep down in the crater. We'll get there eventually, but not anytime soon; the elevator itself is out of order, and we'll need to find a [Power Cell]() in order to get it moving again.

> #5 — Powering Up

You may also happen to notice that at the bottom of the elevator is another habitat, which we'll call Facility Beta, and behind it another automated mining drill. All of that is very exciting but out of our reach until we get some upgrades going, and as Roland mentioned before, that's going to require us to get back onto ARC's trade network.

For now, scout out the rest of the area immediately around Alpha and maybe do a little mining on the nodes that are close by. When you've got a full inventory, grab that Cargo Drone from inside Alpha and let's head home. Exit through the new "Side Cave Entrance" (take the front door out of Alpha, turn NNE, and follow the very faint path) so you can watch Roland eat a little bit of crow for telling you to ignore it when we first came in (during [Arrival]()).

Your next goal is to build yourself a [Trade Node]() in order to interface with the aforementioned trade network. That requires two [Basic Electronics]() from the Crafting Bench, which will cost you 10 [Silica](). You ought to have collected that much Silica from blowing up the rockfall, and you hopefully found one Electronics board in Alpha, so go practice crafting for a bit and build yourself a Trade Node. Surprise! You also need to build a [Comms Antenna](), but the game didn't give you the blueprint for that until you finished the Trade Node. Looks like you need some more [Basic Electronics](), and that means you need more [Silica]()... and that means we're heading back underground.

Before you do, though, a note on the UI: you can "pin" crafting recipes and construction blueprints to the left-hand side of the HUD. This is handy if you have a particular thing you're trying to build and you need to remember what raw ingredients you need in order to build it.

Since you're looking for Silica, your primary targets should be the white Kalynite nodes, or if you strike out a little further down the hill, the light blue Kaloxite nodes. They're both much harder to crack than the orange Regolith nodes. If you're persistent (and enable hold-to-interact mode instead of click-to-interact mode in Settings) you *should just* be able to open them up. If not, you can always try tossing Mining Explosives. Just remember to stand back so you don't catch yourself in the blast.

When you need air, you can either stop near the Side Entrance, or run back to Alpha. When your inventory gets full, dump everything into the Storage Containers and keep going. We've already talked about staging, but when you have large Containers like these to use as a storage point, one really nice thing is that everything stacks inside the container so it's easier to prioritize what you bring back to your Outpost.

Once you have enough raw materials to put together the [Comms Antenna](), head back to your outpost and build it. You're immediately connected to the ARC planetary network... and who's that extremely bored voice on the other end of the line? Why, it's our Best Friend, Trudy, also known as our former ARC Supervisor and now apparently our liaison on the trade network. Fabulous.

To be fair, she immediately does you a solid in gifting you $500 in market credit, which is enough to purchase your first upgrade: *Mining Laser Crystal 1*, which doubles the power of Roland's mining laser and makes it much easier to cut through the nodes you found outside Alpha. You also get a plot hook involving a robot uprising from a century ago that resulted in the death and destruction of an entire megacorp, which seems like an awfully big deal for Roland to be cracking cheap jokes about.

Unfortunately, once again the game has tricked you; you've only unlocked half of the trade network. We can purchase upgrades, but without the Market, we aren't able to buy or sell anything. To do that we'll need to build a [Drone Pod]() and outfit it with a [Cargo Drone](). We did find a drone already in Alpha, so keep that handy. Forging [Kalynite Bars]() and [Building Materials]() ought to be second nature by now, so that just leaves... a bunch more [Basic Electronics](). Yeah, that figures.
